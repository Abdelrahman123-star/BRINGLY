# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Everyday consumers and apparel shoppers seeking fashion, clothing, and accessories. Shoppers want effortless browsing of garments and collections with high-resolution imagery and direct personal inquiry via WhatsApp. Store owners manage inventory and handle customer consultations, pricing quotes, and orders through WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose
A curated personal sourcing showroom and catalog for authentic European and German goods hand-carried to Egypt. Rather than displaying fixed prices and impersonal checkouts, it showcases in-stock pieces cleanly and connects shoppers directly with the store via WhatsApp for private inquiries, custom orders, and requests before upcoming travel flights.

## Positioning
A bespoke personal sourcing and shopping experience. The storefront acts as a digital lookbook and showroom where all purchases and pricing inquiries happen via direct, high-touch WhatsApp communication.

## Operating Context
Mobile and desktop shoppers browsing curated goods and custom sourcing requests. When interested in an item, a single tap opens a WhatsApp conversation with the product pre-filled for immediate consultation.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Discovery & Showcase: Editorial campaign hero banners, collection showcases, and curated product cards without public prices.
- WhatsApp Direct Ordering: Direct WhatsApp action buttons with pre-filled product inquiries on every product card and detail view.
- Germany & European Sourcing Concierge: Custom request form for acquiring authentic goods from Germany with direct WhatsApp follow-up and hand-carried delivery in Egypt.
- Localization: Bilingual English and Arabic presentation.
- Technical environment: Node.js and Express backend with EJS templating, MongoDB.

## Brand Commitments
- Store identity: "BRINGLY" (برينجلي)
- Interaction channel: WhatsApp Direct Concierge (`+20 100 670 9258`)
- Voice: Personal, trusted, authentic, and modern.

## Product Principles
1. Visual Purity: Clean product presentation free of distracting price tags, aggressive sale banners, or commercial popups.
2. Direct Personal Touch: Every item connects directly to a human consultation via WhatsApp.
3. Tactile Heritage: Editorial banners and materials celebrating natural Mediterranean and Egyptian fabrics.
4. Seamless Mobile Experience: Rapid one-tap WhatsApp transition on smartphones and desktop web.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Fully responsive across all screen sizes, accessible button labels, and high contrast typography.
