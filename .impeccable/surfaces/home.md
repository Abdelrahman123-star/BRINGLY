# Surface brief: Storefront Home

## Direction contract
THESIS: A tailored fashion atelier storefront that rejects cookie-cutter card grids and loud commercial popups in favor of calm, material-grounded Mediterranean/Levantine elegance where fabric quality, silhouette, and craftsmanship take center stage.
OWN-WORLD: Palette of Linen White (#FBF9F5), Deep Indigo (#1A2238), Warm Terracotta (#C46849), and Sand/Bone (#F0EAE1, #E8DFD1). Hairline dividers, natural woven textures, tactile fabric tags, generous negative space, and serene bilingual typography (Playfair Display and Plus Jakarta Sans).
STORY: The shopper immediately feels the heritage and tactile craft of the apparel line, browses curated collections with fluid visual continuity, explores product stories, and adds items to their bag with zero cognitive friction.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Hairline header with bilingual brandmark (BRINGLY | برينجلي), serene collection navigation, and discreet bag counter. Full-bleed editorial campaign hero with warm atmospheric framing, clear typography, and a distinct primary action into the latest seasonal drop.
FORM: candidate 3 of grounded list, seed:8743c3e5782d3ac1.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
